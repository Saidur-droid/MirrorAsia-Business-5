## Identity

Business: MirrorAsia — Business 5  
Category: human-calibrated population simulation / synthetic consumer research  
Initial geography: Bangladesh  
Initial wedge: FMCG concept testing  
Expansion: Indonesia → Vietnam → Singapore sales → Gulf  
Primary inspiration: Simile  
Secondary validation: Aaru

## Core thesis

MirrorAsia is not a persona generator.

It creates persistent synthetic consumer populations calibrated against real human respondents.

Core loop:

real humans  
→ structured population data  
→ synthetic twins  
→ simulations  
→ real-human benchmark  
→ error measurement  
→ recalibration  
→ improved population.

## Why customers pay

They pay to:
- test more concepts before field research,
- reduce research cycle time,
- compare many combinations,
- decide which concepts deserve real-human validation,
- get segment-level insight quickly,
- reuse the same calibrated population repeatedly.

## Critical trust rule

Never present a simulation as guaranteed market truth.

Every material output should expose:
- confidence,
- calibration freshness,
- closest benchmark,
- known error,
- population version,
- human-validation recommendation where needed.
