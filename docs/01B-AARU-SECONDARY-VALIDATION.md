# Aaru — Secondary Category Validation

Aaru is kept separate from Simile on purpose.

MirrorAsia is **Simile-first**.

Aaru exists in this repository mainly to prove that synthetic population / AI research is a broader venture category rather than one exceptional company.

---

## Public category signal

Public reporting in late 2025 described Aaru as:

- founded in the synthetic research / population simulation category,
- using large numbers of AI agents to model populations,
- having enterprise / professional-services relationships,
- raising a large Series A,
- receiving a billion-dollar headline valuation treatment in the round reporting.

TechCrunch also reported that the headline valuation did not necessarily reflect the same blended economics for every share in the round.

Therefore we should avoid oversimplifying the financing.

---

## Why Aaru matters to MirrorAsia

Aaru gives us three useful signals.

### 1. Multiple teams are building this category
Simile is not the only venture-backed attempt.

### 2. Enterprise buyers are interested
Consulting/professional-services relationships support the idea that synthetic research can enter large enterprise workflows.

### 3. Competition will increase
This category will not stay empty.

Therefore MirrorAsia needs local data, calibration and outcome history — not only clever prompting.

---

## Why Aaru is NOT our primary operating model

We have much richer public material about Simile's:

- validation methodology,
- confidence system,
- enterprise case studies,
- customer data model,
- funding rationale,
- product evolution.

Therefore:

**Simile = operating reference.**  
**Aaru = category validation / competitive signal.**
