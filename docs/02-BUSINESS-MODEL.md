# MirrorAsia Business Model

## What business are we in?

Not "AI personas."

We are in:

**population intelligence infrastructure.**

The customer wants to know:

- which concept wins,
- what price feels acceptable,
- which message works,
- how segments differ,
- which idea is worth a real study,
- how confident should we be.

## Customer types

Initial:
- FMCG brands,
- D2C consumer companies,
- agencies,
- market research firms,
- consumer startups,
- regional brand teams.

Later:
- banks/fintech,
- telcos,
- media,
- retail,
- mobility,
- consumer healthcare,
- consulting firms,
- product teams.

## Offer ladder

### Managed Simulation Study
Client gives a brief.

MirrorAsia runs:
- scenario design,
- synthetic simulation,
- segment analysis,
- confidence review,
- optional human verification.

### Enterprise Workspace
Teams run their own repeated simulations.

### Population API
External software queries calibrated populations.

### Human Validation Add-on
Real respondent sample to validate priority results.

### Country/Category Population Build
High-value custom calibration for a market/category.

## Why enterprise pays

Traditional research is powerful but expensive to repeat.

MirrorAsia can let companies test many more variants, then reserve human studies for important finalists.

Economic value can come from:
- fewer bad concepts reaching field test,
- faster iteration,
- reduced research cost per iteration,
- better prioritization,
- faster campaign/product decisions.

## Pricing logic

Do not price on LLM tokens.

Possible commercial drivers:
- number of simulations,
- country populations,
- category datasets,
- users/seats,
- human verification volume,
- API calls,
- custom calibration,
- enterprise support.

## Revenue phases

### Phase 1
Managed studies: highest trust, highest human involvement.

### Phase 2
Annual enterprise subscription.

### Phase 3
API/data access.

### Phase 4
Regional population infrastructure.

## Unit economics

Track:
- model compute cost,
- respondent/panel cost,
- human QA/researcher time,
- data licensing cost,
- storage,
- customer support,
- sales.

Goal:
human cost per simulation should fall while predictive quality remains stable or improves.

## Anti-patterns

Do not become:
- cheap persona generator,
- survey chatbot,
- report-writing agency,
- black-box "AI focus group",
- research replacement claim,
- outcome-guarantee business.

The product is valuable because it blends speed with calibrated uncertainty.
