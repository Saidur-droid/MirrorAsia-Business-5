# GTM, Competitions & Demo Strategy

## GTM principle

Sell:
**more confident iteration before expensive field research.**

Do not sell:
"AI consumers."

## First buyers

- FMCG innovation team,
- brand manager,
- consumer insights team,
- creative agency,
- market research agency,
- D2C founder.

## Initial offer

### Mirror Sprint
Client sends:
- 3–10 concepts,
- target segment,
- price options,
- packaging/creative,
- question.

MirrorAsia delivers:
- simulated result,
- segment differences,
- confidence,
- benchmark reference,
- optional human verification.

## Why competitions can help

For startup competitions/hackathons, MirrorAsia has a strong demo story because it is visual and easy to understand.

Demo:
1. upload two package designs,
2. choose Dhaka Gen Z,
3. run 10k synthetic consumers,
4. show segment result,
5. show confidence/error,
6. click "verify with humans,"
7. show how calibration closes the loop.

The important differentiation for judges:

> This is not fake personas. It is a calibrated system with benchmarked uncertainty.

## What judges may attack

### "Why not ChatGPT?"
Answer:
ChatGPT does not provide calibrated population distributions, local benchmark error, real-human refresh or longitudinal population versioning.

### "How do you know predictions are right?"
Answer:
MirrorScore + blind backtests + human validation.

### "Is synthetic research dangerous?"
Answer:
We do not replace high-stakes research. We expose confidence and use real humans where evidence is weak.

### "Where is the moat?"
Answer:
panel + calibration + benchmark history + outcome data.

## Real-world sales motion

1. offer one managed pilot,
2. benchmark against a previous known study,
3. show where simulation matches/misses,
4. use customer real study for validation,
5. convert to annual workspace,
6. expand to more categories/countries.
