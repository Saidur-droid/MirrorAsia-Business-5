# Growth & Funding Roadmap

## Reference lesson

Simile and Aaru show investors can care deeply about synthetic research/population simulation.

But reference-company funding is category validation, not our entitlement.

## Stage 0 — Research proof

Need:
- 10+ historical benchmarks,
- clear methodology,
- respondent/data access,
- initial population.

## Stage 1 — Product proof

Need:
- working simulation,
- confidence layer,
- benchmark output,
- human check.

## Stage 2 — Design partners

Need:
- 3–5 real customers,
- recurring usage,
- real concept tests,
- evidence of time/cost advantage.

## Stage 3 — Pre-seed story

Potential milestones:
- 2k–5k respondent foundation,
- 100+ simulations,
- MirrorScore with clear methodology,
- 3+ paying/design partners,
- one strong case study,
- repeat usage.

Narrative:

> MirrorAsia is building calibrated digital populations for emerging markets, starting where generic models have weak cultural and behavioral representation.

## Stage 4 — Seed story

Need:
- annual enterprise contracts,
- strong retention,
- repeated studies/month,
- improving benchmark quality,
- higher software gross margin,
- first API customer,
- Indonesia launch or credible pipeline.

## Stage 5 — Regional platform

Need:
- multiple populations,
- shared simulation infrastructure,
- country-specific calibration,
- enterprise regional buyers,
- agency distribution.

## Investor metrics

- ARR,
- ACV,
- enterprise logos,
- simulation volume,
- repeat usage,
- human validation volume,
- benchmark accuracy,
- MirrorScore,
- respondent/panel size,
- cost per validated simulation,
- gross margin,
- customer retention,
- country expansion.

## What kills the funding story

- no real panel,
- generic personas,
- no benchmark,
- overclaiming accuracy,
- one-off research agency economics,
- low repeat usage,
- weak privacy/data governance,
- no expansion beyond Bangladesh,
- accuracy that does not improve with data.
