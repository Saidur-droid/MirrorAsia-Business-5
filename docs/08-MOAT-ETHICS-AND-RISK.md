# Moat, Ethics & Risk

## Biggest risk: wrong prediction

The company dies if customers trust wrong outputs.

So uncertainty is a product feature.

## Moat

### 1. Calibrated local panel
Real respondent history.

### 2. Population graph
Deep segment representation.

### 3. Benchmark suite
Historical tests with known human outcomes.

### 4. Calibration history
How model errors change over time.

### 5. Outcome feedback
Customer later shares actual market result.

### 6. Category specialization
FMCG today; other categories later.

### 7. Regional populations
Bangladesh + Indonesia + Vietnam + Gulf.

## Data ethics

Requirements:
- explicit consent,
- clear purpose,
- anonymization/pseudonymization,
- deletion/opt-out,
- data minimization,
- tenant isolation,
- secure storage,
- no selling raw personal identities,
- no individual-target manipulation.

## Sensitive use cases to avoid initially

- political persuasion/election targeting,
- credit approval,
- insurance pricing,
- individual health diagnosis,
- employment screening,
- targeting based on highly sensitive attributes.

Start with aggregate consumer research.

## Bias risk

Synthetic populations can amplify:
- model stereotypes,
- panel bias,
- urban overrepresentation,
- internet-user bias,
- language bias.

Mitigation:
- stratified panel,
- weighting,
- benchmark by segment,
- disclose under-covered populations,
- confidence downgrade,
- human check.

## Model drift

Population behavior changes.

Mitigation:
- monthly/quarterly refresh,
- population versioning,
- benchmark regression tests.

## Trust principle

MirrorAsia should optimize for:

**useful uncertainty, not fake certainty.**
