# Metrics & MirrorScore

## North star

**Validated Decision Utility**

How often MirrorAsia helps a customer make a faster/better research decision that is later supported by human or market evidence.

Hard to measure perfectly, so support with objective metrics.

## MirrorScore

A composite benchmark, never a vanity score.

Potential components:

### Rank agreement
Does synthetic population choose the same winner as humans?

### Preference error
Difference in share/preference magnitude.

### Price sensitivity error
Direction and elasticity agreement.

### Segment consistency
Does model preserve real segment differences?

### Message/creative agreement
Does winning message match?

### Calibration freshness
How recent is the real-human benchmark?

## MirrorScore should always include scope

Bad:
> MirrorScore = 91

Good:
> Bangladesh urban Gen Z / FMCG beverage concept tests / Q2 2027 / n benchmark studies = 18 / MirrorScore = 91

## Confidence engine

Inputs:
- benchmark similarity,
- sample size,
- calibration age,
- segment coverage,
- category coverage,
- historical error,
- scenario novelty.

Outputs:
- high confidence,
- experimental,
- human validation recommended.
