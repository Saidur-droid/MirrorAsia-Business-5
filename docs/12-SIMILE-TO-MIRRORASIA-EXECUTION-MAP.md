# Simile → MirrorAsia Execution Map

This document converts the Simile reference into a practical company-building sequence.

---

# Phase 0 — Research authority before scale

## Simile lesson
The founding team came from deep generative-agent / human-centered AI research.

## MirrorAsia version
We cannot manufacture Stanford credentials.

We can build local authority with transparent empirical work.

Need:

- a published MirrorAsia evaluation methodology,
- blind historical backtests,
- human/synthetic comparison,
- segment bias analysis,
- data/consent methodology,
- reproducible benchmark reports.

Target output:

> "Bangladesh FMCG Synthetic Population Benchmark v1."

---

# Phase 1 — One enterprise problem

## Simile lesson
Public case studies show high-value decisions and earlier research workflows.

## MirrorAsia version
Start with:

**FMCG concept + price + packaging exploration.**

Customer should be able to bring:

- 10–30 concepts,
- target cohort,
- historical research if available,
- optionally first-party customer data.

MirrorAsia returns:

- comparative simulation,
- segment differences,
- confidence,
- benchmark reference,
- human-check recommendation.

---

# Phase 2 — Validate against known truth

## Simile lesson
Simile emphasizes real-human validation and held-out outcomes.

## MirrorAsia version

Before claiming prediction capability:

- collect 10–20 historical studies with hidden outcomes,
- run simulations blind,
- freeze outputs,
- reveal human result,
- calculate error,
- publish methods.

Do this by category.

No cherry-picking.

---

# Phase 3 — Design partners

Target:

- 2 FMCG companies,
- 1 research/creative agency,
- optionally 1 consumer-tech company.

Offer:

- managed simulation,
- high-touch research support,
- human check,
- clear data/privacy agreement.

Ask for:

- repeated use,
- historical benchmark data,
- permission for anonymized case-study metrics if possible.

---

# Phase 4 — Enterprise land-and-expand

Land with:

**concept screening.**

Expand to:

- pricing,
- packaging,
- communication,
- campaign,
- customer journey,
- category competition.

Do not add categories merely for demo variety.

---

# Phase 5 — Customer-specific populations

Allow enterprise customers to add:

- CRM/loyalty data,
- survey archives,
- segment definitions,
- purchase history where lawful,
- campaign response,
- product telemetry.

Create:

**MirrorAsia Bangladesh Base + Company Data = Custom Mirror Population.**

This is the Simile BYO-data lesson adapted to our market.

---

# Phase 6 — Confidence becomes product

Every result must expose:

- population version,
- benchmark count,
- nearest comparable task,
- expected error,
- confidence tier,
- last calibration date.

Customers should know **when not to trust us**.

That is a competitive feature.

---

# Phase 7 — Move from managed service to software

Early:
researcher-supported.

Later:
self-serve scenario workspace.

Then:
API.

Human work per simulation should decline.

Research quality must not.

---

# Phase 8 — Country expansion

Only expand after Bangladesh has:

- strong benchmark suite,
- repeat enterprise users,
- stable economics,
- credible case studies.

Then:

### Indonesia
Build local panel/calibration from scratch.

Do not translate Bangladesh personas.

### Vietnam
Repeat.

### Singapore
Use as regional enterprise sales market.

The product sold in Singapore can be Indonesia/Vietnam/Bangladesh populations.

---

# Phase 9 — Multi-agent systems

Only after single-population predictive quality is strong.

Then model:

- consumer ↔ consumer influence,
- consumer ↔ brand,
- competitor response,
- channel response,
- price changes,
- policy/environmental changes.

This is closer to Simile's larger market-simulation vision.

---

# Funding milestones tied to execution

## Bootstrap / competition stage

Need:
- working demo,
- 1 population prototype,
- 3–5 blind benchmarks,
- visible confidence layer.

Goal:
win credibility, not valuation.

## Pre-seed

Need:
- 10–20 blind benchmarks,
- 2k–5k respondent foundation or credible data access,
- 3 design partners,
- 1–3 paying pilots,
- first MirrorScore,
- repeat usage.

## Seed

Need:
- annual contracts,
- strong retention,
- dozens/hundreds of recurring studies,
- software gross-margin direction,
- customer-specific populations,
- strong benchmark accuracy by scope,
- one second-market proof.

## Series A-style story

Only later.

Need:
- clear regional category leadership,
- multi-country data moat,
- enterprise pull,
- strong growth,
- API/platform usage,
- expanding use cases,
- research authority.

The Simile funding sequence is inspiration, not a schedule.
