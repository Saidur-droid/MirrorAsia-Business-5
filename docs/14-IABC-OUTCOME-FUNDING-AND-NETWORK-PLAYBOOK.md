# MirrorAsia × IABC 2026
## Outcome, Funding, Investor, NRB & Pilot-Customer Playbook

**Purpose:** Use the International AI Builders Congress as more than a competition.  
**Goal:** Convert the event into a pipeline for prize opportunity, investor access, incubation, NRB support, pilot customers, advisors, media visibility and long-term startup growth.

> Important: Nothing in this document should be interpreted as guaranteed funding. The Congress can create access and introductions, but MirrorAsia still has to earn investor interest, customer trust and follow-up opportunities.

---

MirrorAsia should not enter IABC only to "win a prize."

2. **Cash prize opportunity**

12. **Post-event regional expansion connections**

The event should be treated as a concentrated business-development window.

# 4. NRB collaborator strategy

NRB = **Non-Resident Bangladeshi**.

An NRB collaborator is not automatically an investor.

Their highest value is usually:

- technical expertise
- global standards
- product strategy
- customer introductions
- market knowledge
- investor introductions
- credibility
- international expansion support

## Ideal MirrorAsia NRB profiles

Best-fit backgrounds:

### Tier A
- market research / consumer insights
- FMCG innovation
- AI evaluation
- synthetic data / agent simulation
- enterprise AI product

### Tier B
- VC / startup ecosystem
- Singapore / Indonesia / Vietnam business
- data privacy / governance
- growth / enterprise sales

### Avoid
- "advisor" with no actual availability
- person who only wants title
- person with no relevant experience
- person whose contribution cannot be documented

## Our ask to an NRB

Suggested message:

> We are building MirrorAsia, a human-calibrated population simulation platform for emerging markets. We are entering the International AI Builders Congress 2026 and are looking for an NRB collaborator who can actively advise us on one of three areas: AI/evaluation architecture, enterprise consumer-insight strategy, or regional market expansion. We are not looking for a name-only advisor. We want a real contributor who can review the product, challenge our assumptions, and help us reach global standards.

# 5. Investor-ready story

MirrorAsia should not pitch itself as:

> "Bangladesh's version of Simile."

Better:

> "MirrorAsia is building the calibrated population intelligence layer for emerging markets, starting with Bangladesh FMCG research and expanding country by country."

The story:

### Problem
Consumer research is expensive, slow, and difficult to repeat across many concepts.

### Why now
AI can simulate many scenarios quickly, but generic models are not trustworthy enough for local market decisions.

### Product
MirrorAsia combines:

**real human panel data → synthetic populations → simulation → benchmark → recalibration → confidence**

### Wedge
FMCG:
- price testing
- packaging
- concept
- message
- promotions

### Moat
- local respondent panel
- Bangla/Banglish data
- calibration history
- benchmark suite
- prediction-vs-human error history
- category-specific population knowledge
- enterprise outcome data

### Expansion
Bangladesh → Indonesia → Vietnam → Gulf / other emerging markets.

### Business model
Managed studies → Enterprise SaaS → Population API.

# 7. Pilot-customer ask

The best event ask for FMCG / research professionals:

> "We are not asking you to buy immediately. Give us one historical concept test or packaging decision where you already know the human result. We will run MirrorAsia against it and show exactly where the system matches or fails."

This is powerful because:

- customer risk is low,
- we get benchmark data,
- we can prove calibration,
- we learn failure cases,
- we can potentially convert to a paid pilot.

### Pilot target

By the end of the Congress cycle, try to secure:

- 3 design partners
- 1–2 actual benchmark datasets
- 1 paid or serious pilot discussion

# 12. Funding conversation framework

If an investor asks:

### "How much are you raising?"
Do not invent a number before a real budget and milestones exist.

Answer:

> We are currently defining the smallest pre-seed round required to reach a defensible benchmark dataset, 3 design partners and repeat enterprise usage. We would rather size the round around those milestones than choose a vanity fundraising number.

### "Why will this become big?"
Answer:

> The core software can be reused globally, while each market adds a proprietary calibrated population layer. That creates a compounding data and benchmark moat across countries.

### "Why can't OpenAI / Simile do this?"
Answer:

> They can enter the market, so our moat cannot be "we use AI." It has to be proprietary local panels, longitudinal benchmarks, category-specific calibration, enterprise outcome history and fast country refresh.

# 13. Prize strategy

Treat prize money as a bonus.

Do not build the company around winning.

If prize money is won, suggested priority:

1. respondent panel / calibration data
2. benchmark studies
3. product infrastructure

- unnecessary office
- vanity marketing
- nonessential features
