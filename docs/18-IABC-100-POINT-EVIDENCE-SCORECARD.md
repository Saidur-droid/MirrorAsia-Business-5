# IABC 100-Point Evidence Scorecard — MirrorAsia
**Version:** 1.0 · **7 November 2026**  
Use this as the single review sheet before every demo rehearsal and submission freeze.

| Judging criterion | Weight | What a judge should see | Evidence required | Current honest status |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Innovation | 20 | A reusable Bangladesh population layer, category adapters, a human-evidence feedback loop and scope-aware uncertainty | Source registry, population schema, held-out benchmark design, at least one real category benchmark | Strong thesis; population still mostly illustrative; no real consumer benchmark |
| Technical Execution | 20 | Working full flow: define decision → select population → run simulation/AI analysis → compare with evidence → expose uncertainty | Live deployment, reproducible run, API/schema, tests, model/prompt provenance, security and rollback | Live Studio and AI critique; research submission UI/production binding still incomplete |
| Business Model + Global Readiness | 20 | Buyer-specific decision and ROI; clear service-to-SaaS/API path; country-pack strategy | 5+ discovery conversations target, 2 qualified design-partner candidates, pilot proposal, pricing tests | Hypotheses/docs exist; customer validation not evidenced |
| Real-World Impact + Ethical AI | 20 | Consent, data minimisation, withdrawal/retention, bias/coverage, no false certainty | Approved consent flow, privacy review, response-quality report, bias/coverage and confidence gates | AI safeguards exist; actual human evidence absent; survey product not yet production verified |
| Scalability + NRB Collaboration | 10 | Shared core with modular category/country packs; real external collaboration | Architecture, tenancy/API plan, cost/latency evidence, documented collaborator role and contribution | Architecture direction documented; collaborator not verified |
| Presentation | 10 | A compelling 180-second real product workflow and candid limitations | 1-page summary, architecture graphic, real screen recording, 3-minute video, accessible demo | Demo product exists; final video/submission receipt not verified |

## Rehearsal questions
1. What decision can the user make faster or better?
2. Which parts are measured data, which are synthetic, and which are LLM-generated?
3. What evidence grounds this exact population and category?
4. What happens when the model is wrong or the benchmark is stale?
5. What is the buyer's current alternative and why would they pay?
6. Which category/segment is actually validated today?
7. What did the team build independently, and what third-party sources/tools were used?

## Red-line claims
Never claim: national representativeness, real consumer validation, calibrated prediction, MirrorScore, customer traction, paying customers, partnership, NRB contribution, or funding unless the evidence register contains the corresponding primary evidence and a reviewer approves the claim.

## Release sign-off
A criterion is “ready” only when a reviewer can open the linked artifact and reproduce or inspect the claim. A feature checkbox without evidence does not count.
