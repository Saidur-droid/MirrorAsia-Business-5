OUR RESEARCH DIRECTION

Human evidence
belongs at the centre.

The goal is not to replace people with a convincing answer. It is to explore more possibilities, measure where synthetic results miss reality, and improve against genuine human benchmarks.

Explore scenarios→Test with people→Measure the gap

Human benchmarking and calibration are planned. The current demo uses illustrative assumptions.

TRANSPARENCY, NOT THEATRE

A useful preview.
Not a market forecast.

Mirror Asia is an early research prototype. Its current outputs are synthetic and uncalibrated. They are not evidence of purchase intent, sales or real-world consumer preference.
